About this evaluation

HMM emissions use the classifier’s candidate-arc scores. Viterbi decoding combines those scores with road-network connectivity and distance between observations.

Displayed accuracy and F1 use route-alignment-derived proxy labels. They are not independently verified ground truth.

Metrics are calculated per trip. Select a trip above to inspect its route and scores.
